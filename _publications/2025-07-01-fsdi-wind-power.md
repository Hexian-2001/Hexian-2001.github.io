A novel frequency-aware Transformer for wind power forecasting. FSDIformer proposes **frequency-sparse attention** and **downsampling interaction blocks** to model noisy, non-stationary wind-power series and capture short- and long-range temporal dependencies. Benchmarked against Informer, Autoformer, iTransformer, TimesNet, and TimeMixer on measured wind-farm data, it improved errors by **11.87%** for ultra-short-term and **5.97%** for short-term forecasting over the second-best model. First author; Journal Impact Factor: 10.1.
