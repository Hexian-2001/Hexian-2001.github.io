I am a meteorological foundation model algorithm engineer and a weather & power forecasting researcher. My work sits at the intersection of **weather foundation models**, **renewable-energy power forecasting**, and **large-scale AI systems for weather and energy**. I develop foundation models for wind and solar forecasting, and build the data, training, inference, and deployment pipelines that turn them into operational decision support.

I am currently with **Ming Yang Smart Energy Group** (Ming Yang Smart Energy Group Beijing Technology Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of SSE: 601615), where I develop weather forecasting foundation models for renewable-energy applications.

## Research Interests

- Weather foundation models
- Renewable-energy power forecasting
- Time-series foundation models
- Probabilistic forecasting
- AI agents for energy systems
- Large-scale AI systems for weather and energy
