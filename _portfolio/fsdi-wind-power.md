First author and lead developer. FSDIformer is a frequency-aware Transformer designed for wind power forecasting.

- Proposed **frequency-sparse attention** and **downsampling interaction blocks** to model noisy, non-stationary wind-power series and short- and long-range temporal dependencies.
- Benchmarked against Informer, Autoformer, iTransformer, TimesNet, and TimeMixer on measured wind-farm data.
- Improved errors by **11.87%** for ultra-short-term and **5.97%** for short-term forecasting over the second-best model.
